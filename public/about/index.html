A train horror game about getting home

You play Maya, heading home alone after a late shift. Her phone is almost dead. She texts her mom, who replies the way moms do: text me when you get home.

Then the walk. A deserted street with the station about to close. An empty subway station with flickering green lights and a long escalator down. A platform where the gates slam shut behind you, and a man standing in the dark with his eyes closed. When the last train finally pulls in, you get on, and so does he.

There are no monsters and no gore. Last Train is realistic, situational horror: the kind of fear that comes from being stuck on a subway at night with a stranger who keeps ending up next to you.

What you do

  • Race the clock. Get to the station before it closes, then down to the platform before the gates come down.
  • Watch him without being seen. On the platform he only moves when you look away.
  • Do what people really do to feel safe. Stand where the CCTV camera can see you. Stay in the light. Text a friend, fake a phone call, hold your keys between your fingers.
  • Keep your fear down. The closer he gets, the louder your heartbeat, the darker the edges of the screen and the slower you move, until your legs won't move at all.
  • Decide when to get off. When the doors open at the first stop, get off early or stay on with him until yours. The ending depends on it.

How to play

On a computer

W to walk, Shift to run, the mouse to look around, C to sit or stand, E for the actions that appear on screen.

On a phone

Hold it upright. Thumbstick under your right thumb to walk, drag with your other thumb to look, and tap the buttons that appear on screen.

Questions

Is Last Train free?

Yes. It's free and plays in your web browser, with nothing to download or install.

How long is it?

About 5 minutes from start to finish.

Can I play it on my phone?

Yes, on phones held upright. It plays best on a computer with headphones.

What kind of horror is it?

Realistic, situational horror. No monsters, no gore: you're alone at night on the subway and someone is following you.

Is there a content warning?

Yes. Last Train depicts fear, stalking and themes of sexual violence. No assault is shown. Support resources are listed at the end of the game and in its menu.
